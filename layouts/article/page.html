{{- /* An article: a research post, a day of a trip, a short trip. Every regular page in `research/` and `trips/` is one, because the section's `_index.md` cascades `type: article` onto its pages — which is what makes Hugo pick this file. A page that should be built from bricks instead (the thesis page) says `type: page` in its own front matter. Articles do not go through `section_map.html` the way an ordinary page does: they are one article rather than a stack of bricks, and what makes them one is where they live, not what is written in them. So the `post` brick renders the page whole; the links to the neighbouring posts and the call to action every post closes on are added after it. The call to action is a shared brick, written once per section: `content//bricks/cta-
.md` when there is one, the generic `bricks/cta.md` otherwise. Its content has to go through `inline_attributes.html` here, because the page being rendered is not the one the markdown came from: `section_map.html` hoists the attribute lists for an ordinary page, and nothing has hoisted these. */ -}} {{ define "sections" }} {{- partial "sections/post.html" (dict "Content" .Content "Name" "post" "Index" 0 "Page" .) -}} {{- partial "site/postnav.html" . -}} {{- with or (site.GetPage (printf "/bricks/cta-%s" .Section)) (site.GetPage "/bricks/cta") -}} {{- partial "sections/cta.html" (dict "Content" (partial "inline_attributes.html" .Content) "Name" "cta" "Index" 1 "Page" $) -}} {{- end -}} {{ end }}
